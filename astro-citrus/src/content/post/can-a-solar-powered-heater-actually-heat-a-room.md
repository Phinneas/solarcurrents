A solar powered heater may seem like a convenient and eco-friendly solution for heating a room, but its limitations are often overlooked. While it's true that some portable solar heaters exist, they have real limitations that make them unsuitable for serious cold. A genuinely portable solar space heater that provides meaningful warmth in real cold is not yet a practical product at reasonable cost.

## How It Works

A solar powered heater uses solar panels to charge a battery, which then powers a low-wattage heater. This setup is commonly seen in portable solar space heaters designed for small spaces like a van, tiny cabin, or greenhouse. However, the key limitation lies in the unit's ability to provide consistent heat. On a sunny day, it can heat a room for a few hours, but as the sun sets, the heater's performance drops significantly.

## Common Mistakes

One common mistake people make when considering a solar powered heater is to assume that it can provide meaningful warmth in cold temperatures.
