## Buying Criteria for Portable Solar Panels

When evaluating any portable solar panel, you must move past the marketing claims of "maximum power" and look at the real-world physics. First, look at the cell efficiency. A higher efficiency rating means a greater percentage of the captured sunlight is converted into usable electricity. Second, consider the power density, which is the power output divided by the panel's weight or surface area. This is the true measure of how good a lightweight solar panel is. I would advise against buying solely based on the advertised wattage without checking the dimensions, as manufacturers often inflate power numbers under perfect Standard Test Conditions (STC) that rarely occur in reality. Finally, check the operating temperature range. If your intended use involves extreme cold or heat, a narrow operating range can render the panel useless even if it's light.
