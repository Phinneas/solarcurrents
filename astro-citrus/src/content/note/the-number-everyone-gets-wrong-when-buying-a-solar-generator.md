Watt-hours (Wh) is the number that actually matters when [sizing a solar generator](/posts/solar-generator-sizing-calculator/) — not watts. Watts tells you how fast power flows. Watt-hours tells you how much total energy the battery holds.

A 1,000W generator with a 500Wh battery can run your 100W device for about 4 hours (accounting for efficiency losses). A [500W generator with a 1,000Wh battery](/posts/solar-power-bank-vs-portable-power-station/) runs it for roughly 8.

Most buyers fixate on peak watts. Most regrets come from [undersized batteries](/posts/lifepo4-vs-nmc-battery-lifespan/).
