Every solar sizing guide hedges. Your location, usage patterns, [battery chemistry](/posts/lifepo4-vs-nmc-battery-lifespan/), temperature, and panel angle all genuinely change the numbers.

But "it depends" becomes useful when you pin down the variables that matter most for your situation:

- Where are you using it? (latitude + typical cloud cover)
- What are you powering? ([watt-hours per day](/posts/solar-generator-sizing-calculator/), not just peak watts)
- How many cloudy days do you need to survive on [battery alone](/posts/how-home-solar-power-banks-work/)?

Answer those three and the math gets a lot less ambiguous.
